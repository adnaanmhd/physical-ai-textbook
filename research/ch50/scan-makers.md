# Humanoid makers beyond the starting list (scanned 2026-10-07)

## What is new since 2025
The field is wider than the 19-lab list. Three groups stand out.

1. Industrial incumbents and carmakers now build or buy humanoids: Xiaomi, XPeng, Samsung (with Rainbow Robotics), Mobileye (buying Mentee), Schaeffler (a customer, and an actuator supplier to two makers).
2. Wheeled "humanoids" (Hexagon AEON, Humanoid's HMND 01 Alpha Wheeled, Sunday's Memo) are the ones with named factory or home pilots. Legged robots are mostly still demos or R&D sales.
3. Data strategy keeps splitting. Sunday uses gloves worn in homes. Xiaomi uses handheld UMI devices (over 100K hours). Mentee says it trains in simulation only. Hexagon, Schaeffler and Persona rely on customer-site production data.

All numbers below are company claims unless marked.
