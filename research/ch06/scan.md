# ch06 scan · Transmissions, joints and sensing the joint (scanned 2026-10-06)

## What is new since 2025
- 1X published NEO's 25-DoF hand (2026-07-09): motors in forearm, tendons through the wrist, quasi-direct-drive ratios of ~5:1 to 15:1, backdrivable, wrist torque 17.75 N·m, millions of test cycles claimed.
- Agility's Digit 5 moves to cycloidal leg actuators (2026-09-15).
- Schaeffler's CES 2026 actuator is a two-stage planetary design that stresses smooth back-driving.
- Academic work on parallel elastic ankles (Tsagarakis group, arXiv 2608.30832, 2026-08-31) and roller-screw knees continues.
- Tesla Gen 3 tendon hand claims: patent/blog only; unconfirmed.

## Surprises and controversies
- The "roller screw vs rotary" debate is mostly marketing and blogs; peer-reviewed comparisons are thin.
- 1X's 5:1-15:1 "QDD tendon" claim is a company claim, not independently tested.
- Gaps: harmonic and cycloidal drive primaries (Harmonic Drive, Nabtesco), encoder and torque-sensor datasheets, variable-stiffness actuator review.
