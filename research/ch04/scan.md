# ch04 scan · Anatomy, degrees of freedom and the reference humanoid (scanned 2026-10-06)

## What is new since 2025
- Boston Dynamics showed the product electric Atlas at CES (2026-01-05): 56 DoF, 90 kg, 1.9 m, 50 kg peak lift, 4 h battery, self battery-swap.
- Agility unveiled Digit 5 (2026-09-15): 129 kg, 50 lb lifts, 90-minute battery with a 9-minute charge, cycloidal leg actuators; early access H1 2027.
- Unitree H2 (31 DoF, 182 cm, ~70 kg) is listed at $29,900.
- Apptronik showed Apollo 2 (June 2026) with no published spec table; Apollo 3 teased for 2027. [press only; not yet primary]
- Tesla has NOT unveiled Optimus V3 as of mid-September 2026 (Q2 update, 22 Jul: "later this year"). Gen 3 figures online (22-DoF hands, 173 cm, 57 kg) come from blogs and patents: not usable as specs.
