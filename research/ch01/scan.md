# ch01 scan · The lifecycle on one page (scanned 2026-10-06)
## What is new since 2025
- Three WAM/world-model surveys appeared in 2026; WAM is now a named survey category.
- Foundation models now span whole-body humanoid control (Helix 02, Gemini Robotics 2), not just arms.
- pi0.7 pushes "steerable" prompting with metadata and subgoal images.
- Deployment results are now reported (Figure/BMW, Agility/GXO), which supports the readiness-and-flywheel framing.
