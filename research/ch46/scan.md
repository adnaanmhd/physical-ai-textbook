- BMW announced Figure 03 for Spartanburg logistics (June 2026), after a Figure 02 pilot said to support 30,000+ X3s. Pilot length is reported as 10 or 11 months; sources differ.
- Hexagon AEON moved from a Leipzig test (Dec 2025) into production tasks (June 2026), targeting full deployment by end-2026. No metrics published.

- 1X NEO: $20,000 or $499/month. Delivery status unverified by me.
