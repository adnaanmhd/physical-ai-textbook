# ch45 scan · Standards, certification and regulation (scanned 2026-10-06)
## What is new since 2025
- ISO 10218-1/-2:2025 published (Edition 3); Part 1 folds in ISO/TS 15066 content and adds cybersecurity (per ISO search listing).
- ISO 25785-1 (dynamically stable industrial mobile robots) is still a Committee Draft, per ISO's page. Not published.
- ANSI/A3 R15.08 series reported complete with Part 3 (2026); A3 TR R15.108 for humanoids is in development (per Agility).
- ISO 13482 revision reported near FDIS: unconfirmed, ISO page returned 403.
- EU: the Digital Omnibus on AI (Reg. 2026/1744) in force 27 Jul 2026 pushes AI Act high-risk dates to 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I products); Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 still applies 20 Jan 2027.
- China issued a Humanoid Robot and Embodied Intelligence Standards System (2026 edition), 52 initial standards, 28 Feb 2026.
- Agility says Digit passed an OSHA-recognised field evaluation (company claim).
