# ch51 scan · Where deployment is happening, and where it's next (scanned 2026-10-06)
## What is new since 2025
- Automotive: Figure reports an 11-month BMW Spartanburg run (sheet-metal loading). Boston Dynamics opened its Atlas training centre at Hyundai's Georgia plant on 2026-09-21. Hyundai's stated plan: real Atlas deployment from 2028, assembly by 2030.
- Logistics: Agility reports 100,000+ totes at one GXO site, and a Toyota Canada commercial RaaS deal (Feb 2026, via secondary report; primary unconfirmed).
- Homes: 1X opened NEO pre-orders (Oct 2025); aggregators found no verified customer delivery as of July 2026. Teleoperation is part of the model.
- Elder care: Donut Robotics' humanoid has patrolled a Japanese nursing home since July 2026; trial is small.
- Market size: IFR counts about 7,000 full-size humanoids sold in 2025, almost all in China and mostly for R&D or entertainment (secondary reports; IFR release does not state it).
