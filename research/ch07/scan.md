# ch07 scan · Hands and end-effectors (scanned 2026-10-06)
## What is new since 2025
- Figure 03 (2025-10-09) added palm cameras and in-house fingertip sensors.
- Figure's Brett Adcock posted an F.04 hand preview on 2026-10-03. It exists, but Figure has published no DoF, actuator, tactile or payload figures (per the RuntimeWire report; the X post itself was not opened).
- Boston Dynamics showed a new Atlas hand on 2026-10-02: 13 DoF, four fingers, direct actuation, tactile fingertips and palm, power-tool use. Only secondary reports were read; the primary post (blog "Robot Hands for Modern AI and Real Work", URL unconfirmed) is still to find.
- Tesla: tendon-driven 22-DoF hand patents surfaced in April 2026. Musk reportedly posted on 2026-04-19 that the design "didn't actually work". The Optimus hand is a moving target, so this is unconfirmed and needs a primary source.
- Sharpa Wave: 22 DoF, 20 N fingertip force, 500 Hz, about 1,000 tactile pixels per fingertip.

## Suggested sections
Gripper vs hand; DoF and actuation (direct vs tendon vs hydraulic); sensing in the hand; strength vs delicacy; durability; matching human demonstrations; tools.
## Surprises and controversies
Two leading firms (Figure, Tesla) reportedly abandoned their tendon hands. Boston Dynamics chose four fingers. Dexterity claims have no independent benchmarks. Gap: Shadow, Psyonic, Inspire and Sanctuary were not scanned.
